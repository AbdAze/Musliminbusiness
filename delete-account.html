MIB Account Deletion Request

Use this form to permanently delete your MIB account and all associated personal data. Please read the information on this page carefully before proceeding.

What Happens When You Delete Your Account?

Submitting this form initiates a formal account and data deletion request under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Our team will verify your identity and process the request within 7–14 business days.

Once deleted, your MIB membership, business profile, connections, conversation history, and all associated data will be permanently erased from our servers. This action cannot be undone.

1

Submit the Deletion Form

Fill in your registered details accurately so our team can verify your identity and locate your account.

2

Identity Verification

Our privacy team will cross-verify your submitted details with the account on record within 1–3 business days.

3

Account & Data Erasure

Upon successful verification, your account and all personal data will be permanently deleted within 7–14 business days.

4

Email Confirmation

You will receive a confirmation email to your registered address once the deletion is complete.

📌 Certain records such as transaction history may be retained for up to 7 years as required by Indian financial regulations, even after account deletion. For any queries, contact mibindiaoffice@gmail.com.
